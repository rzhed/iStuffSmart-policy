PRIVACY POLICY — iStuffSmart
Last updated: September 2026
Developer contact: rzhed@protonmail.com

SUMMARY
iStuffSmart is a private, offline-first home inventory app. We do not collect,
transmit, sell, or share your personal information. There is no account to
create, no server that stores your data, and no advertising or tracking. Your
inventory, photos, and receipts stay on your device.

1. WHAT iStuffSmart STORES, AND WHERE
Your inventory items, rooms, categories, tags, photos, receipts, attachments,
drafts, reminders, and your free-plan item usage count are stored locally in
iStuffSmart's private app storage on your device. iStuffSmart does not upload
your inventory to any server, because it has no server to upload to.

2. NO ACCOUNT
iStuffSmart has no account, no sign-in, and no user ID. We cannot identify you,
and we have no database of you.

3. PURCHASES AND YOUR APPLE ACCOUNT
If you subscribe to iStuffSmart Premium, Apple processes the purchase, payment,
and subscription status through the App Store using your Apple Account. Apple
shares with us only the verified product and entitlement information needed to
unlock Premium. iStuffSmart never receives or stores your full payment-card
details, your Apple Account password, or your billing address. Apple handles
refunds, billing disputes, and payment issues under its own policies.

4. EXPORTS AND SHARING — THE ONLY WAY DATA LEAVES YOUR DEVICE
Inventory leaves your device only when you explicitly export or share a file, or
when you print or save a document. Exported files can contain the records you
choose to include, including names, photos, prices, and notes. Share them only
with people and services you trust. Once a file leaves the app, its handling is
between you and your chosen destination. We have no access to it.

5. PERMISSIONS
Camera and photo-library access is requested only when you choose to attach a
item photo or receipt. Notification permission is requested only when you enable
reminders. Denying any permission does not prevent you from using the rest of
the app.

6. ANALYTICS, ADVERTISING, AND TRACKING
iStuffSmart contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics trackers, and
no cross-app tracking. The app does not use the App Tracking Transparency
framework because it does not track. We cannot see how you use the app beyond
what stays on your device.

7. CHILDREN
iStuffSmart is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect
information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. INTERNATIONAL DATA AND TRANSFERS
Because we do not collect your data, there is no data to transfer across
borders. Apple processes subscription payments globally under its own privacy
policy.

9. RETENTION AND DELETION
Your data exists only on your device. It is removed when you delete individual
items, when you use "Delete All Data" in Settings, or when you uninstall the
app. "Delete All Data" removes your inventory, photos, receipts, shopping list,
tags, and usage ledger from the device; it does not cancel an Apple
subscription, which you manage through Apple's subscription settings. Because
the free-plan usage count is also stored on the device, uninstalling and
reinstalling resets that count. Removing an item does not return a free-plan
slot.

10. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES
Because we do not collect your personal information, there is nothing for us to
disclose, correct, or delete on our side. You have full, immediate control: view,
edit, export, and delete everything inside the app at any time, with no request
to us and no waiting period.

11. SECURITY
Your data is stored in iStuffSmart's private sandbox on your device, protected by
the operating system's standard protections (your device passcode, data
protection, and app isolation). It is not transmitted over a network, so it
cannot be intercepted in transit. Your exported files inherit the security of
wherever you choose to save them.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this policy as the app changes, for example if a future version
adds a feature that transmits data. The date at the top always reflects the
current version, and the same text is available inside the app under
Settings -> Premium -> Privacy Policy.

13. CONTACT
Questions about privacy can be sent to rzhed@protonmail.com. We respond to
privacy questions from individuals.
